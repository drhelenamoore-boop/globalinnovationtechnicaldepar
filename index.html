Our Vision & Mission

Driven by Purpose

We believe that talent is equally distributed, but opportunity is not. The Global Innovation Fund bridges this gap by investing directly in low‑income individuals and community‑led projects that have the potential to create lasting social and economic change.

Since our founding, we've supported over 500 ventures across 30 countries, focusing on sustainable agriculture, clean energy, digital skills, and small‑business incubation. Our approach is transparent, inclusive, and built on trust.

Who Can Apply

Individuals & Teams

Applicants from underserved communities with a clear project idea or early-stage venture.

Small Business Owners

Existing micro or small enterprises seeking growth capital or innovation support.

Nonprofits & Community Groups

Registered organizations working on local empowerment initiatives.

General requirements: 18+ years of age, low-income background (self-declared), and a project aligned with sustainable development goals.

How to Apply

  1. Submit Your Idea
    Fill out our simple online form describing your project, its impact, and how funding will be used.
  2. Review & Due Diligence
    Our team of experts reviews applications based on feasibility, community benefit, and sustainability.
  3. Interview & Proposal
    Shortlisted applicants are invited for a friendly virtual chat to discuss their vision in detail.
  4. Funding & Mentorship
    Approved projects receive grants ranging from $500 to $25,000, plus 12 months of mentorship.
